59  Finale: formulating and planning your own tool

The week-thirteen kickoff, modeled live.

NoteDraft stub — planned contents

A student formulates a small tool that is useful in molecular biology, something their own background lets them pose, understand, and sanity-check, and plans it from a scaffolded blank page: deciding the pieces, naming them, stating inputs and outputs, and writing the first tests before prompting anything. Describes the floor, a tool over the supplied gene CSV held in a dataframe, and the ceiling, a tool built on a public resource such as BioPython or a REST API. Refers to the dataframes note and the documentation note.
