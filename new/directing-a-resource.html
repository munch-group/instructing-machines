24  Directing to resources: clinic

A companion to the week-twelve clinic on building on machinery you did not write.

Directing the AI to use a resource:

NoteDraft stub — planned contents

Works a small example live: reading a piece of a library’s or an API’s documentation, deciding which function or endpoint is wanted, instructing the assistant to use it, and verifying the result against the documentation and a test. The first modelled instance of building on machinery the student did not write, and the rehearsal for project D and the finale ceiling.
